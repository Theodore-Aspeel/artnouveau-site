# ANAD 2.0 - 90-day execution roadmap

Date: 2026-09-16  
Owner: Christophe Aspel  
Operating mode: small reviewed batches, human approval at publication and commercial gates

## Strategic sequence

ANAD should first become a reliable publication system, then a repeatable social
acquisition system, and only then a commercial system. Monetization before editorial
and measurement foundations would weaken the author-led positioning.

## Days 1-7 - Close Recovery

- validate the mobile editorial pilot at 390 and 768 px;
- merge the evidence-bound Article + Place JSON-LD contract;
- record the preview-host decision;
- accept the publication-readiness inventory as migration baseline;
- update and close Recovery.

Exit only with green deployment profiles, no reported mobile overflow, no unsupported
structured-data claim and explicit next human decisions.

## Days 8-18 - Complete Maison Coilliot end to end

- write six localized alternative texts for two supporting photographs;
- complete factual and FR/EN/NL editorial checks;
- approve image framing and order;
- assign the real publication date and status;
- generate and approve the social package;
- document one complete research-to-social run.

Human approval remains mandatory for facts, article, web result and social package.

## Days 19-35 - Make publication control real

- review the six `READY_STRUCTURE` articles in small batches;
- finish alternatives for the five accessibility cases;
- resolve Bratislava and Ernest Delune factual exceptions;
- decide what to do with the unidentified Charleroi facade;
- add the production filter only after every intended live page is `published`;
- test that future drafts stay out of build, gallery, sequences and sitemap.

## Days 22-45 - Build the SEO and knowledge layer

- confirm Search Console ownership and indexation baseline;
- record query and landing-page performance;
- review entity contracts for city, architect and movement;
- add first editorially useful internal links;
- monitor JSON-LD after deployment;
- do not auto-generate thin city or architect pages.

## Days 36-56 - Produce one Reel pilot

- define the ANAD 9:16 visual grammar;
- create a Maison Coilliot storyboard and three hook alternatives;
- render a restrained 15-30 second video from original photography;
- add subtitles, caption, CTA and a traceable target URL;
- obtain human approval before publication;
- decide on a deterministic template only after the pilot.

Compare three-second retention, average watch time, completion, shares, saves and
profile visits with the account’s recent median, not generic viral thresholds.

## Days 45-65 - Connect Instagram and the site

- adopt one UTM convention for bio, Reel and Story links;
- align the CTA from Reel to profile to article;
- keep the article-to-Instagram path discreet;
- add a campaign landing page only if measured behavior justifies one.

## Days 50-75 - Install the learning loop

- produce one weekly Metricool + Search Console decision report;
- define the first `performance_report` contract;
- observe three content cycles before automating recommendations;
- record every evidence-based change in GitHub.

Do not add Airtable or Notion as a second source of truth merely to display the same
workflow.

## Days 70-90 - Run one restrained commercial experiment

Choose one reversible test only after traffic baselines exist: guide/newsletter
interest, contextually relevant disclosed affiliation, or partnership enquiry.

Before launch define editorial separation, disclosure copy, one conversion goal and
a stop condition if trust or reading metrics decline. Avoid generic display ads,
aggressive pop-ups and an affiliate catalogue.

## Tool and model policy

Use GitHub for source and decisions, browser/device checks for QA, Search Console for
organic discovery, Metricool for social performance, Canva for approved social assets
and Remotion only if the Reel pilot proves a template useful.

Use Luna for mechanical inventories, Terra for implementation and tests, Sol for
orchestration/editorial/SEO decisions, and Astra only for an exceptional cross-system
problem that Sol cannot resolve reliably.

## Execution checkpoint - 20 September 2026

- scope, launch calendar and first Reel creative recommendation are documented;
- the unified read-only `pipeline-status` contract identifies the first unfinished
  stage and exact next action for each article;
- Maison Coilliot is technically ready through editorial QA, EN/NL localization
  and image rights;
- the next calendar gate remains Christophe's preview review and selection of a
  real publication date;
- no Reel render, Instagram connection or performance automation begins before
  the corresponding human approvals.

## Do not do early

- framework or CMS migration;
- bulk status changes;
- mass-generated SEO pages;
- automated social publication before several approved pilots;
- ten Reels before learning from the first small batch;
- a paid SEO suite or additional database without measured need;
- AI-generic visuals replacing original photography.
