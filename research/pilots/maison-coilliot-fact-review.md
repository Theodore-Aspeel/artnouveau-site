## Review purpose

This packet separates source-backed facts from the article's visual interpretation.
It does not change the public article, assign a publication date or move the article
out of `draft`.

## Primary source

  [Maison Coilliot et son décor intérieur](https://www.pop.culture.gouv.fr/notice/merimee/PA00107670),

The Ministry notice states that it reproduces the terms of the legal protection
order and is authoritative for the protected property. It is the controlling source
for identity, address, construction campaign, architect and protection status.

## Public-copy review

The existing article makes no factual claim that conflicts with the official notice.
Its attribution to Guimard is deliberately cautious even though the source supports
the stronger designation `architect`.

Most of the body is first-person visual criticism about rhythm, threshold, relief,
movement and the facade's relation to the street. Those passages are editorial
interpretation and should remain identifiable as such. They are not historical facts
derived from the Ministry notice.

One sentence should be reconsidered during the final French edit: `Même lorsque la
documentation reste partielle`. The official documentation is sufficient for the
core identity, date, architect and protection facts. If retained, the phrase should
clearly refer to the article's intentionally limited on-site account, not to a lack of
authoritative documentation.

The sentence `Il comprend que l'ornement n'a pas été ajouté après coup`, and its EN/NL
equivalents, reads like an unsupported construction chronology. POP supports an
integrated-design interpretation but does not establish when every ornamental element
was installed. Rephrase it as a visual perception, for example that ornament seems
indissociable from the building's perceptible structure.

The comparison with L'Huîtrière and the Lumière du Nord lodge is an editorial route
through Lille. It must not be read as evidence of a historical relationship among
the three buildings.

## Quotation verification

The short Hector Guimard quotation is supported by the Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian
Design Museum article
[Hector Guimard: Art Nouveau to Modernism](https://www.cooperhewitt.org/2023/02/09/hector-guimard-art-nouveau-to-modernism/),
consulted 2026-09-16. The museum article identifies the original source as Guimard's
1902 article in *Architectural Record*, volume 12, number 2, page 127.

The quote is relevant to Guimard's design principles but is not a statement about
Maison Coilliot specifically. The current attribution preserves that distinction.
Cooper Hewitt supports the English wording. The French version in the article is an
editorial translation and must be approved as a translation, not presented as a
French-language archival quotation.

## Claims not approved from this packet

Do not infer or publish from the current evidence:

- a single exact completion day or month;
- a claim that the interior can be visited;
- current ownership, occupancy or opening conditions;
- a direct historical connection between Maison Coilliot, L'Huîtrière and the
  Lumière du Nord lodge;
- additional material, restoration or biographical detail not stated in the cited
  sources.
